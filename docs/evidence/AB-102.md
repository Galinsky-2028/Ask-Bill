## Scope implemented and paths
- `tools/perf/load.mjs`: spend-safe reader load harness (public GET routes only, no cookies or generations), remote-target opt-in, request/concurrency caps, p50/p95/p99, status counts, budget verdict and exit code.
- `tests/load-harness.test.mjs`: guard rails (remote flag, forbidden API/admin/metrics/query paths, caps, credentialed URLs), budget verdict on injected latency/errors, real burst against the demo runtime.
- `docs/capacity/READER_LOAD.md`: usage, local baseline and unmeasured scope.

## Results
