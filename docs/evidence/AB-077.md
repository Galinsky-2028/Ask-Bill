## Follow-up (character runtime v2)

Still a candidate asset only; **no art unit (AB-045 to AB-062) is claimed**, and the stage stays **off by default**.

- **AB-063 WebGPU path:** `pickBackend` uses WebGPU only when a real adapter exists and the renderer reports a WebGPU backend; otherwise, or on any WebGPU failure, it falls back to a fresh WebGL 2 canvas. `ASK_BILL_CHARACTER_BACKEND=auto|webgpu|webgl2` (default `auto`). three.js `three.webgpu.min.js` is served same-origin with its own GLTFLoader variant, so the loader shares the WebGPU build's classes. WebGPU device loss is handled like WebGL context loss.
- **AB-064 facial layers** (`packages/character/animation/face.mjs`): a seeded blink scheduler (2.5–6 s intervals, occasional double blink) acting on the re-pivoted eye parts; critically damped gaze that offsets iris and pupil toward cited text; procedural viseme weights on `MouthVisemes`, clamped and normalized (total ≤ 1), active only while verified speech plays or an answer is explained. Extra clips are mapped: think (answering), explain (first validated segment), nod (supported answer), shrug (no supported answer), greet, curious.
- **AB-072 follow mode** (`packages/character/renderer/follow.mjs`):
  - Wide screens: the guide panel is sticky, so Bill stays visible inline.
  - Non-sticky layouts: Bill walks into a compact dock only when an empty side gutter can hold it (never over content), and returns inline when the slot is visible again.
  - `ASK_BILL_CHARACTER_FOLLOW=false` disables the dock.
- **AB-060/063 golden poses and parity:**
  - `tests/browser/test_character_golden_poses.py` renders Idle, Point, Talk and WalkFunny at fixed times through the production stage code (`renderPoseSnapshot`).
  - Renders are compared with goldens in `tests/fixtures/character-golden/` (tolerance 3 % of foreground pixels; measured 0 % locally), and WebGPU is checked against WebGL 2 (tolerance 12 %; measured about 5 %).
  - The test also proves that blink plus gaze visibly changes the eyes (measured 3.9 %).
- **Browser test:** `tests/browser/test_character_stage_browser.py` now runs the full stage under forced WebGL 2 and under WebGPU (SwiftShader Vulkan). It checks the sticky follow and the gutter dock (dock left edge ≥ content right edge).
- **Accessibility:** axe with the stage enabled and running reports 0 violations at 1280 px and 375 px. The nested landmark was fixed: the stage is a labelled group, not an aside.
